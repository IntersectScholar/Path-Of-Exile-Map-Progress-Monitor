## 🎯 What It Does

A lightweight, on‑the‑fly monitor that watches your PoE map‑completion data and draws clear, real‑time visualisations of the territories you’ve cleared and the ones that still await you. Keep your Atlas tidy, spot gaps instantly, and plan your next league run with confidence.

---

## ✨ Key Features

| ✅ | Feature | Why It Matters |
|---|---------|----------------|
| **Live Sync** | Reads the game’s client files while you play (no manual export). | No interruptions – your progress updates automatically. |
| **Interactive Atlas** | Zoom, pan, and hover over individual maps for detailed stats. | Spot missing maps at a glance. |
| **Progress Summaries** | Percentage‑filled, remaining maps, and per‑region breakdowns. | Quickly gauge how close you are to a full clear. |
| **Export Options** | Copy summary to clipboard or save as a plain‑text report. | Share your achievements on forums or Discord. |
| **Custom Themes** | Light & dark modes, plus a “League‑Ready” colour palette. | Match your desktop aesthetic. |
| **Low‑Footprint** | Built with .NET 6, < 30 MB install, negligible RAM impact. | Runs smoothly alongside the game. |

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. **Run the app** – it will automatically locate your PoE installation folder.
2. **Allow read access** – the monitor needs permission to read the `LeagueData` folder (a single click in the prompt).
3. **Start a game** – the dashboard updates as you finish each map.
4. **Explore the Atlas view** – click any region to see detailed completion numbers.

> **Tip:** Pin the app to your taskbar for one‑click access while you’re grinding.

## 🛠️ Technical Details

- **Language:** C#
- **Framework:** .NET 6 (Windows Desktop)
- **UI Library:** WinUI 3 (modern native look)
- **Data Source:** Reads `LeagueData\MapProgress.json` generated by the official client.

The source is intentionally closed‑source to protect the parsing logic, but the binary distribution is fully signed and verified.
